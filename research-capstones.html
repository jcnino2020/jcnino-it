MSME financial tracking & microloan readiness

Research-led system concept

Research question
How could clearer income, expense, and cash-flow records help an MSME owner understand their financial readiness?
Method & scope
Proposed approach: map financial-record workflows, prototype a tracking dashboard, and evaluate whether intended users can interpret the summaries. This is a proposed evaluation plan, not a report of completed participant research.
Documented contribution
The portfolio concept organizes income and expense tracking, cash-flow summaries, business profiles, and readiness indicators into a possible web-system workflow.
Current evidence & findings
No participant findings, loan eligibility outcomes, or validated readiness scores are published. The project remains a concept/prototype in this portfolio.

MSME inventory & consumer access

Capstone concept

Research question
How could inventory visibility and product availability information reduce uncertainty between local businesses and consumers?
Method & scope
Proposed approach: map stock-update and product-discovery tasks, prototype business and customer views, then evaluate task completion and clarity with intended users. No completed study is asserted.
Documented contribution
The portfolio concept connects product records, stock alerts, availability status, customer-facing information, and administrative summaries.
Current evidence & findings
No measured sales gains, customer adoption, or completed usability-study findings are published. The current contribution is the documented system concept.

HydroAlert river awareness

Public simulation prototype

Research question
How can an interface explain upstream observations, downstream context, and field reporting while keeping simulated data clear?
Method & scope
Implementation review of the public prototype: inspect monitoring, community, and field-report flows; identify labels distinguishing synthetic readings and local storage. This is an interface review, not a validated hydrology study.
Documented contribution
The public prototype connects scenario controls, synthetic station readings, a community view, and browser-local field reports. Its architecture and data model are documented in the system-design collection.
Current evidence & findings
Observable result: separate views and local report persistence are implemented. No flood-prediction accuracy, warning lead time, sensor validation, or community-impact result is claimed.
