# MSME financial tracking & microloan readiness

Status: Research-led system concept
Updated: 3 November 2026

## Question
How could clearer income, expense, and cash-flow records help an MSME owner understand their financial readiness?

## Method and scope
Proposed approach: map financial-record workflows, prototype a tracking dashboard, and evaluate whether intended users can interpret the summaries. This is a proposed evaluation plan, not a report of completed participant research.

## Documented contribution
The portfolio concept organizes income and expense tracking, cash-flow summaries, business profiles, and readiness indicators into a possible web-system workflow.

## Current evidence and findings
No participant findings, loan eligibility outcomes, or validated readiness scores are published. The project remains a concept/prototype in this portfolio.
