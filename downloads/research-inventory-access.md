# MSME inventory & consumer access

Status: Capstone concept
Updated: 3 November 2026

## Question
How could inventory visibility and product availability information reduce uncertainty between local businesses and consumers?

## Method and scope
Proposed approach: map stock-update and product-discovery tasks, prototype business and customer views, then evaluate task completion and clarity with intended users. No completed study is asserted.

## Documented contribution
The portfolio concept connects product records, stock alerts, availability status, customer-facing information, and administrative summaries.

## Current evidence and findings
No measured sales gains, customer adoption, or completed usability-study findings are published. The current contribution is the documented system concept.
