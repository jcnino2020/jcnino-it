# HydroAlert river awareness

Status: Public simulation prototype
Updated: 3 November 2026

## Question
How can an interface explain upstream observations, downstream context, and field reporting while keeping simulated data clear?

## Method and scope
Implementation review of the public prototype: inspect monitoring, community, and field-report flows; identify labels distinguishing synthetic readings and local storage. This is an interface review, not a validated hydrology study.

## Documented contribution
The public prototype connects scenario controls, synthetic station readings, a community view, and browser-local field reports. Its architecture and data model are documented in the system-design collection.

## Current evidence and findings
Observable result: separate views and local report persistence are implemented. No flood-prediction accuracy, warning lead time, sensor validation, or community-impact result is claimed.
